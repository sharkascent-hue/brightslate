How we clean a roof

    Scrape off the moss by hand

    Before you book

    Good questions, straight answers.

    Something else? Call or text 087 555 0192.

    Will cleaning damage my tiles?

    Not the way we do it. Moss is scraped off by hand and the roof gets a gentle low-pressure soft-wash. High-pressure blasting is what damages tiles and forces water underneath, so we never use it on roofs. The pressure washer only comes out for driveways and patios.

    How long does the roof stay clean?

    The soft-wash kills the spores left in the tiles, so most roofs stay clear for three to five years. Every roof clean comes with a 2-year moss-free guarantee: if it grows back in that time, we come back and treat it for free.

    Do I need to be home?

    No. We just need access to the front and back, and an outside tap if you have one. We text when we arrive and send photos when we're finished.

    What happens to all the moss?

    We bag it and take it away, and clear the gutters and downpipes so nothing blocks later. Paths, windows and cars nearby get rinsed before we leave.

    What if it rains on the day?

    The soft-wash needs a few dry hours to work. If the forecast turns, we move you to the next dry day at no cost and text you the new date.
